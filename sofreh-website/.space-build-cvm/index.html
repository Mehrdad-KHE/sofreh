Questions, answered straight

What is Sofreh?

Sofreh is a Toronto marketplace where certified home cooks sell real Persian home cooking — ghormeh sabzi, tahchin, zereshk polo and more — to people in their own neighbourhood. You browse today's dishes, order ahead, and pick up from a cook nearby or get local delivery.

Where does Sofreh serve?

The pilot serves Richmond Hill, North York, Thornhill, Scarborough, downtown Toronto and Mississauga. Pickup from the cook is always free; delivery runs within each cook's own neighbourhood.

Are the cooks food-safe?

Every cook must hold a valid Ontario Food Handler Certificate, list all ingredients and allergens on every dish, and cook in a kitchen that passes Sofreh's onboarding checklist. Dishes are sealed and labelled with the cook's name, ingredients and preparation time.

How much does it cost?

The dish price plus a 10% service fee capped at $4. Pickup is free, delivery is a flat $4.95 in the pilot area, and cooks keep 85% of the dish price. The full total appears before you confirm.

Can I cancel?

Yes — free cancellation any time before the cook confirms preparation has started. After that the dish is being made fresh for you, but you can message the cook through Sofreh to rearrange pickup.

How do I become a cook?

Apply from this page with your neighbourhood and signature dishes. You need an Ontario Food Handler Certificate. Sofreh takes 15% commission, pays weekly, and you choose your menu, prices and cooking days.

About Sofreh

Sofreh is a neighbourhood marketplace for Persian home cooking in Toronto, Canada. It was founded by Mehrdad Kheirollahi, a bookkeeper and long-time Torontonian, after years of watching talented home cooks in the Iranian community cook for family while neighbours searched the city for food that tasted like home.

The name comes from the sofreh — the cloth spread on the floor or table where a Persian meal is shared. Sofreh the marketplace works the same way: a cook in Richmond Hill, North York, Thornhill, Scarborough, downtown Toronto or Mississauga posts the dishes they are making, and people nearby order a single portion or a family tray for pickup or local delivery. Every cook is certified, every dish lists its ingredients and allergens, and every price — including fees — is public before anyone pays.
